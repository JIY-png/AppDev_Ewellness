Import Health Records (CSV)

Upload a CSV file containing health records with patient vital signs and information.

CSV format with headers: Date, Time, FirstName, LastName, Age, Gender, BloodPressure, HeartRate, OxygenSaturation, BodyTemp, Weight, Height, BMI

Health Records CSV Format

Standard Format (with Verbose Headers):

Date,Time,FirstName,LastName,Age,Gender,BloodPressure,HeartRate,OxygenSaturation,BodyTemp,Weight,Height,BMI
